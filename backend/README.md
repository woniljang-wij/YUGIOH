# Yu-Gi-Oh! account server

## Duel engine status

The heuristic duel AI is implemented in `duel_ai.py` as a mixin of `DuelActionEngine`; it uses the action engine for game-rule operations.

Monarch Bóng Tối (Caius) can only target the opponent's on-field cards. Its Tribute Summon is not blocked when the opponent has no cards; in that case, the summon completes and the effect is skipped.

Ryko, Lightsworn Hunter resolves its Flip effect on Flip Summon or after battle flips: it destroys one selected field card, then mills up to three cards from the opponent's Deck.

Heavy Storm Duster can be activated after being Set, selects one or two Spell/Trap Cards on either field, and destroys the selected cards.

Gandora cannot be Special Summoned; its activated effect pays half the controller's LP, destroys and banishes other field cards, and gains 300 ATK per card destroyed. It is sent to the Graveyard in the End Phase if Normal, Tribute, or Flip Summoned that turn.

Chú Hề Ma Quái (Crass Clown) targets one opponent's monster and returns it to its owner's hand when Normal, Flip, or Special Summoned.

Crass Clown's summon trigger is handled by the duel engine; a Fusion Monster returns to its owner's Extra Deck.

Tricky can be Special Summoned from the hand during a Main Phase by choosing and discarding one other card from the hand; the server validates the cost and available Monster Zone.

Lightning Storm can be activated only when the opponent controls at least one monster or Spell/Trap; the player then chooses once per turn between destroying the opponent's face-up Attack Position monsters or their Spell/Trap cards.

Sao Chép Ma Thuật and Đá Ma Thuật Phát Sáng let the player choose and pay their two-card discard cost before the Spell enters the Chain. Only cards already in the player's Graveyard before paying the cost are eligible; the discarded cards cannot be selected. Activation is rejected when no eligible card exists. After resolution, Sao Chép Ma Thuật can recover a pre-existing Spell, while Đá Ma Thuật Phát Sáng can recover any pre-existing card.

Hoàng Đế Raider can be Special Summoned directly from the hand during a Main Phase when its controller has no monsters; it gains 500 ATK after destroying a monster by battle, and the monster that destroys it loses 500 ATK. Phù Thủy Thời Gian can be activated once per turn in a Main Phase to toss a coin; the side with a picture destroys all monsters the opponent controls, while the other side destroys as many monsters as possible under its controller's control and inflicts damage equal to half the ATK of the destroyed face-up monsters.

Kẻ Trộm Mộ can be activated after being set to take one selected card from the opponent's Graveyard into its controller's hand; the card's original owner remains unchanged.

Người Đá Dung Nham can be Special Summoned to the opponent's field by Tributing two monsters they control. Its summoner cannot Normal Summon that turn, and its controller takes 1000 damage during each of their Standby Phases.

Cấp Số Nhân Tributes a face-up Kuriboh, then Special Summons a Kuriboh Token in Defense Position to each available Monster Zone. Tokens disappear when they leave the field and can still be Tributed for a Tribute Summon.

Quan Tài Của Phù Thủy lets the player choose and Tribute one of their monsters, choose a Spellcaster from their hand to Special Summon in face-up Attack Position, then optionally target and destroy one opponent's monster.

Mèo Ma Thuật can optionally add one Spell from its controller's Deck to their hand when it is destroyed and sent to the Graveyard; the player chooses a Spell or skips the effect.

When Siêu Ma Gilfer is destroyed and sent to the Graveyard, it randomly selects one of the opponent's face-up monsters and reduces that monster's ATK by 500 until it leaves the field. If the opponent has no face-up monsters, the effect does nothing.

Rồng Trắng Mắt Xanh Thể Rắn negates one selected face-up opponent's monster when Normal or Special Summoned; the negation lasts while that monster remains on the field. Its once-per-turn Quick Effect can respond to an opponent's card or effect activation during either player's turn: shuffle this card into its owner's Deck, then Special Summon a Blue-Eyes White Dragon from the activating player's Deck if available.

Jinzo is handled as a continuous engine rule rather than an activated registry handler; Monarch Băng Giá has a validated Tribute Summon choice of up to two on-field Spell/Trap Cards, and Monarch Bóng Tối can complete its Tribute Summon without an opponent's monster, then selects another on-field card to banish and inflicts 1000 damage if it was a DARK monster. Its summon is not blocked when no other valid target exists. This is a port of the existing subset, not complete coverage of every skill in `cards.json`. Catalog descriptions are authoritative; for example, Dark Magic Curtain summons without an LP cost. Rồng Hỗn Mang Levianeer is implemented in the summon/trigger flow rather than the effect registry: banish exactly two LIGHT and/or DARK monsters from the Graveyard to Special Summon it from the hand, then resolve the matching LIGHT-only revival, DARK-only random hand shuffle, or mixed-attribute destruction of up to two field cards. Its summon effect is once per turn, it cannot attack that turn, and it cannot be revived before being properly summoned. The mixed-attribute effect lets the player choose fewer than two targets or skip destruction.

Rồng Chữ Y can be equipped from the hand or field to a face-up ĐẠI BÁC Cannon X you control once per turn. It grants 400 ATK/DEF, replaces that monster's destruction in battle or by card effect, and can be unequipped for a Special Summon on a later turn. Xe Tăng Bọc Thép Z can similarly equip to a face-up ĐẠI BÁC Cannon X or Rồng Chữ Y, grants 600 ATK/DEF, and provides the same destruction replacement and later-turn detach Special Summon. If either equipped monster leaves the Monster Zone, its Union equip is sent to the Graveyard.

Bạch Thạch Cổ Đại can optionally Special Summon a Blue-Eyes White Dragon from its owner's hand or Deck when destroyed by battle and sent to the Graveyard. Its Main Phase Graveyard effect banishes itself to add a Blue-Eyes White Dragon from the Graveyard to the hand. Both effects share one use per player per turn.

Thiếu Nữ Mắt Xanh can Special Summon a Blue-Eyes White Dragon from the hand, Deck, or Graveyard when an opponent activates an effect targeting it. When targeted for an attack, it can negate the attack, change its battle position, and optionally Special Summon a Blue-Eyes White Dragon from those zones. The two effects share one use per player per turn.

The authenticated `POST /api/duel/start`, `GET /api/duel/state`, `POST /api/duel/action`, and `POST /api/duel/abandon` routes manage one in-memory match per user. The React duel now starts from the saved deck on the server and renders its public snapshot; drawing, phase progression, Normal/Tribute/Flip Summons, setting monsters or Traps (including validated Tribute Sets), changing battle position, attacks, supported effect activation, Chain passing, pending discards, and end-turn hand limits are submitted to the action API. Local state is limited to selection and presentation. Attack resolution checks direct-attack legality, monster ATK/DEF comparisons, face-down defenders, battle protections, and registered attack Traps. The AI draws, resolves supported Standby effects, evaluates and activates beneficial registered Spells, performs multiple Main Phase actions, sets available back row, and enters the Battle Phase. It attacks directly only when the opposing Monster Zone is empty, attacks an attack-position monster when its ATK matches or exceeds the target's ATK, attacks a defense-position monster only when its ATK exceeds the target's DEF, and may attack a face-down monster without using its hidden stats to choose the target. Its response to Chain Links remains heuristic. At match start, the AI builds a 40-card singleton deck with 22 monsters, 11 supported Spells, and 7 supported Traps, choosing a Dark Magician or Blue-Eyes core and filling the remaining slots with playable monsters and general utility cards from the catalog. Public state hides the AI's hand and deck contents, and opponents' unrevealed face-down cards. Card effects remain a partial catalog port; unsupported effects are rejected rather than resolved by the UI. The in-memory match is lost when the backend restarts. Fusion Summoning remains deferred; Extra Deck cards may be represented in state but are not summoned by this engine.

The Flask app serves the frontend and its same-origin authentication API. It connects to the SQL Server instance configured in `backend/.env` (default: `.\SQLEXPRESS`) using Windows Authentication by default, then creates or verifies the `YUGIOHDB` database and its player tables during startup. The schema setup adds `PlayerName` and `SuccessfulRenameCount` to existing user tables, initializes names from `Username`, and initializes the rename count to zero; newly registered players also start with their account name as their player name.

Valkyrion the Magna Warrior is a Main Deck Effect Monster. It cannot be Normal Summoned or Set; it is Special Summoned from the hand by Tributing one each of Alpha, Beta, and Gamma from the hand and/or field. Its face-up field effect Tributes itself to Special Summon one each of those three monsters from the Graveyard.

## Quick start (Windows)

After completing the one-time setup below, run `..\run_project.bat` from the project root (or double-click `run_project.bat`). The script checks or starts the configured local SQL Server instance, builds the React frontend, starts Flask in a separate window, and opens the website (normally at `http://127.0.0.1:5000`; it selects the next available port if needed). SQL Server may require administrator permissions to start if its service is stopped.

## Configure

1. Install Python 3.13, Node.js 18 or newer, and Microsoft ODBC Driver 18 for SQL Server.
2. From the project root, create a virtual environment and install the backend and frontend requirements:

   ```powershell
   py -3.13 -m venv .venv-runtime
   .\.venv-runtime\Scripts\Activate.ps1
   python -m pip install -r backend\requirements.txt
   npm --prefix frontend ci
   ```

3. Copy `.env.example` to `.env` and set a long random `FLASK_SECRET_KEY`. `SQL_AUTH_MODE=windows` uses the Windows account running Flask; that account must be allowed to create the database on first run and create tables/indexes in it.
4. If using SQL Server Authentication instead, set `SQL_AUTH_MODE=sql` and add `SQL_USERNAME` and `SQL_PASSWORD` to the local `.env`. Do not commit `.env`.
5. Start the website from this folder:

   ```powershell
   python app.py
   ```

6. Open `http://localhost:5000`. The login/register page is the entry point; after login, use the start screen to enter the game. The game and duel pages redirect to sign-in when no user is logged in. Set `PORT` in `backend/.env` to use a different available port.

The registration API stores Werkzeug password hashes, not plaintext passwords. Player names are updated in SQL Server through the authenticated profile API, not in browser storage. The first rename is free; later renames consume up to ten `rename_card` items according to the persisted successful rename count. `GET /api/profile/rename-info` returns the server-calculated cost and owned quantity, while `POST /api/profile/player-name` validates and commits the name change, card deduction, count increment, inventory event, and idempotent request response in one SQL transaction. SQL values are parameterized, and the session cookie is HTTP-only and SameSite=Lax. For local HTTP development, keep `SESSION_COOKIE_SECURE=false`; set it to `true` when serving over HTTPS.
The selected lobby character is stored per account in `dbo.Users.SelectedCharacterId` and loaded from the authenticated session, so the same character appears across browsers. Existing accounts are initialized to Jonouchi when this column is added; changing characters updates the account through `POST /api/profile/character`.
Player wallets are stored in `dbo.Users.Gold` and `dbo.Users.Gems`. Existing accounts receive 103,000 gold and 3,500 gems the first time the schema migration runs, and the lobby loads the current balances from SQL Server. Card ownership is stored per account in `dbo.PlayerCards`, keyed by user and card ID; the card catalog is stored in `backend/data/cards.json` and returned to logged-in clients by `GET /api/cards`. Images, audio, and video are stored in `backend/assets/` and served at public `/assets/...` URLs because the browser needs those files to render and play the game; moving them into the backend does not make them private. Character-pack cards have a three-copy collection limit; the shop displays remaining copies. Cards in Gói Bài Thần use an individual one-copy redemption limit.

The authenticated wallet endpoint `POST /api/wallet/exchange` accepts `{ "gems": 100 }`, `{ "gems": 1000 }`, or `{ "gems": 6000 }`, converting those amounts to 1,000, 10,000, or 60,000 gold. The gem deduction and gold credit are committed together in SQL Server, and insufficient balances are rejected without changing either balance.

The authenticated shop endpoints are `GET /api/shop` for wallet, packs, and card quantities; `POST /api/shop/purchase` for character packs; `POST /api/shop/redeem-card` for Gói Bài Thần; and `POST /api/shop/reset` with `{ "pack_id": "kaiba" }` to restore a resettable character pack to its initial state. `POST /api/shop/items/rename-card` accepts a quantity and UUID request ID, calculates the 1,000-Gem-per-card price on the server, and commits the Gem deduction, stacked `rename_card` inventory grant, item event, and idempotent response together. Character-pack draws exclude cards already at their three-copy limit; a purchase that cannot be fully supplied is rejected without charging. Resetting removes the account's owned copies from that character pack and restores its available copies; it does not refund spent currency. Wallet deduction, item use, and card grants run in SQL transactions.

The schema initializer also creates `dbo.PlayerCharacters`, `dbo.PlayerInventory`, `dbo.PlayerItemEvents`, and `dbo.PlayerActionRequests`. `rename_card` uses the existing inventory tables and `backend/assets/images/items/rename-card.png`; grants of the same item are stacked by user and item ID. Existing valid character choices and wallet/card data are retained. The selected-character column is made nullable so a new account must choose one of the three character assets before using the shop; only the chosen character is unlocked at level 1. At level 5 the next character in the order Kaiba → Yugi → Jonouchi (cycling after the selected character) is unlocked; at level 10 the remaining character is unlocked. All three are implemented using the actual existing characters and artwork.

`dbo.Users.Experience` and `dbo.Users.Level` hold progression. The EXP thresholds are centrally defined by the backend and included in `GET /api/session` and `GET /api/inventory`. `POST /api/inventory/use` accepts `{ "item_id": "exp", "quantity": 1, "request_id": "<uuid>" }`; each item grants exactly 10 EXP, and usage is rejected at level 10. The transaction log makes repeated requests with the same UUID idempotent. `GET /api/inventory` returns only owned items, their quantities, artwork paths, descriptions, and the most recently recorded positive grant source.

Character cards are stored as `character_card` inventory items and are automatically spent before currency: each card pays for one character pack, and excess cards are retained. Character packs offer 1-, 10-, and 50-pack purchases at 500 gold or 700 gems each; the backend uses character cards first, then charges gold when the remaining gold balance covers the purchase, and otherwise falls back to gems. Each draw applies rarity odds of 70% R, 20% SR, and 10% UR among cards that still have copies available. Each card can be obtained up to three times, matching its `maxCopies` limit. `POST /api/shop/purchase` accepts `pack_count` values of 1, 10, or 50 and a unique `request_id`; payment currency is selected and validated by the backend from the current wallet and inventory. The backend checks the character unlock and commits the inventory deduction, wallet change, item-event record, action record, and card grants together. `POST /api/shop/reset` resets card copies and restores the three-copy availability, but never refunds spent currency or character cards.

Gói Bài Thần is separate from character packs. Its five Exodia cards use `packIds: ["god"]` in `cards.json` without `characterIds`, have UR rarity and `maxCopies: 1`, and require five `ur_fragment` items per card. The three Egyptian God cards and Horakhty are also in this pack, use GR rarity and `maxCopies: 1`, and require five `gr_fragment` items per card. `POST /api/shop/redeem-card` accepts a `card_id` and UUID `request_id`; it locks the account, rejects an already-owned card or insufficient fragments, then deducts the correct rarity fragment and grants exactly one card in the same idempotent SQL transaction. All catalog cards appear in the library; missing artwork uses the built-in placeholder until artwork is added.

The attendance API persists monthly and online check-ins in `dbo.PlayerAttendanceClaims`; successful monthly claims are recorded as mission events in the same SQL transaction. Mission progress and reward claims use `dbo.PlayerMissionEvents`, `dbo.PlayerMissionProgress`, `dbo.PlayerActivityPoints`, and `dbo.PlayerActivityChestClaims`. Daily and weekly mission records persist their period start and end dates; weekly periods start on Monday and end on Sunday using the server's `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` business date. Event keys, account-row locking, unique claim keys, and `dbo.PlayerActionRequests` protect progress and rewards against retries and concurrent claims.

The authenticated mission endpoints are `GET /api/missions`, `POST /api/missions/claim` with a server-defined `mission_id` and UUID `request_id`, and `POST /api/missions/chests/claim` with a server-defined `chest_id` and UUID `request_id`. Successful daily attendance and pack purchases update mission progress inside their existing transactions. Only successful mission claims add activity points; weekly chests grant the configured existing gold, gems, character-card, and EXP items atomically. Activity points are tracked separately and are never added to inventory.

Fragments are regular `dbo.PlayerInventory` items (`r_fragment`, `sr_fragment`, `ur_fragment`, and `gr_fragment`) and use the existing item artwork. `POST /api/fragments/merge` accepts an input `item_id` and UUID `request_id`; the server atomically deducts the required fragments and Gold, grants exactly one next-tier fragment, records item events, and stores an idempotent response. The recipes are 40 R + 1,000 Gold → 1 SR, 20 SR + 2,000 Gold → 1 UR, and 10 UR + 3,000 Gold → 1 GR. GR is the highest tier and cannot be merged further.

`POST /api/cards/dismantle` accepts a `card_id` and UUID `request_id`; it consumes three copies of that exact card and grants one fragment matching its rarity in the same transaction. Copies assigned to any saved deck are reserved and cannot be dismantled. The response includes the updated inventory and remaining owned/available card counts.

Decks use the existing `dbo.PlayerCards` ownership and `backend/data/cards.json` catalog. `GET /api/decks` returns the owned-card pool, the ten deck slots, the saved duel selection, per-deck duel validation messages, wallet balances, and unread eligible card notifications. `POST /api/decks/select` persists an unlocked, duel-valid deck for the signed-in account; duel use requires 40–60 Main Deck cards and respects the existing 20-card Extra Deck, ownership, card-type, and copy limits. `POST /api/decks/save` saves a deck's name, note, Main Deck, and Extra Deck with an optimistic version check; the backend validates ownership, card type, each card's `maxCopies` limit (capped at three), and 60/20-card capacities in a transaction. Copies used in one deck do not count against another deck. `POST /api/decks/unlock` unlocks Decks 2–10 for 1,000 gems each using the server-side price and an idempotency request ID; Deck 1 starts unlocked. `POST /api/decks/cards/seen` marks an eligible new card as viewed for the signed-in account. Deck data is stored in `dbo.PlayerDecks`, `dbo.PlayerDeckCards`, and `dbo.PlayerNewCards`; duel deck selection is stored in `dbo.PlayerDuelPreferences`.
